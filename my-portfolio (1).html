Hello, I’m Wuraola Odunewu.

I’m a Virtual Administrative & Operations Support professional with experience supporting business administration, client coordination, documentation, customer communication and HR-related activities.

My background includes working in a travel and study-abroad support company, providing administrative support to a professional association, and contributing to HR administration through volunteer roles.

Through these experiences, I’ve developed a practical understanding of the work that keeps a business running behind the scenes — from maintaining organised records and managing documentation to coordinating activities, tracking pending tasks and ensuring important follow-ups are not overlooked.

I enjoy bringing structure to everyday work, keeping information organised and helping people stay on top of their responsibilities.

I approach every assignment with attention to detail, clear communication, accountability and a willingness to understand how a business operates before providing support.

My goal is simple: to help growing businesses spend less time worrying about administrative details and more time focusing on the work that moves their business forward.
